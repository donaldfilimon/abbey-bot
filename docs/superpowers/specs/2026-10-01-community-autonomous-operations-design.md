# MLAI community and autonomous operations

Approved intent: Donald requested implementation of the complete in-chat MLAI redesign on 2026-10-01. This document records implementation constraints; it does not claim runtime acceptance.

## Community

Broad AI commons with optional Research and Builder guilds. Rules acceptance grants ordinary participation; introductions are optional. Preserve channel IDs/history, staff boundaries, supporter entitlements, and age-restricted opt-in adult spaces. Research uses source-backed AI/agents, memory/retrieval, evidence selection, compute, integrations, and operator-tools tags. Stable project homes link help, demos, releases, and collaboration. Weekly discoveries and rotating guild activities, monthly showcase; live events require an actual host.

## Abbey

Active companion in explicitly eligible spaces. Participation is independent of learning and durable memory. Initial guild unsolicited budget is six/hour and channel cooldown 120 seconds. Local-only generation and content processing never silently fall back off-host. Unavailable local capabilities stay unavailable. Personal memory writes are member-controlled; moderation records and shared source-linked knowledge are separate. Voice requires individual consent; no automatic raw-audio/transcript retention. Adult behavior never enters general channels.

## Operations

Versioned owner-controlled policy authorizes actions outside model prompts. Daily assessment; five structural operations/day, at most two new channels/day, seven-day same-target cooldown except verified failure recovery. Each step counts. Preserve effective access when moving channels; deny sensitive access changes, privileges, entitlement changes, deletions, and policy self-modification. Ordinary role/access operations must match an explicit approved matrix and current rules acceptance.

Durable receipts contain authority, trigger, intent, before/expected/observed state, outcome, and recovery. Single execution lease; reserve before external mutation. Uncertain outcomes require readback, never automatic replay. Idempotency keys bind exact requests. Stop controls preempt queued work; cancellation cannot pretend a remote mutation was undone. Multi-step changes use compensating operations, not atomic rollback claims.

Contextual moderation remains proposal-only until qualified. Eventual automatic ceiling: remove violating message and timeout <=600 seconds, evidence/explanation/staff notice/appeal, no staff/bot targets, no kicks/bans. Ambiguity goes to staff.

## Settings and acceptance

Classic Discord controls show requested/effective settings, scope/dependencies, current authorization, and persistence receipts. Private exports; separate destructive reset confirmation. Overview, conversation, memory, media/adult, moderation, operations, local models, diagnostics/recovery.

Review all live guild surfaces with explicit dispositions and unknowns. Inventory/access snapshots precede migrations; revalidate drift and read back each batch. Source acceptance uses ./check.sh; memory-contract changes require WDBX conformance. Source, installed identity, provider qualification, live Discord, voice consent, and member journeys are separate evidence layers. No new dependency, commit, push, credential rotation, or additional gateway is authorized implicitly.
